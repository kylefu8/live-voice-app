## Applying for authorization

Contact **kylefu8**. If you can access this repository, you may open a commercial licensing inquiry issue, or use contact channels publicly provided on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/kylefu8).

Describe the using entity, product/project, features used, modifications or redistribution, online service plans, and deployment scope. Scope, duration, price, and delivery terms must be agreed separately in writing. There is no fixed fee schedule or automatic billing here. A donation, payment, repository access, or contribution alone does not grant commercial authorization.
